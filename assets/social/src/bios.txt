## youtube (1000)
Roamix is an iPhone app that replays where you have been. Bring in a Google Timeline export, your photos and your Health workouts, and every trip comes back as a 3D flythrough, a timeline you can scrub, a short reel and a living map of the world.

It is not a service. There is no Roamix server, no account, no analytics and no ads. Your location history stays on your phone, and if you turn on iCloud sync it goes to your own private iCloud, not to us.

The app is free, all of it. The one thing we sell is a 3D-printed plaque of a trip, $59 with free US shipping.

Made by one person in Kansas.
