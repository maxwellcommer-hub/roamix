Nothing to sign up for

No profile, no analytics, no ads. Maps and place names come from Apple Maps, so the map areas involved go to Apple.

One exception: if you order a plaque, we receive what it takes to make it and post it to you — your name, email and address from checkout, and that one trip’s route, rounded. Nothing else from your library comes with it.
