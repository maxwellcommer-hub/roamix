Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE 12 AUGUST 2026 · MAXWELL COMMER

Roamix is a private travel journal that runs on your iPhone. It reads your photos, location, and Health workouts to rebuild your trips — and none of it is ever sent to us, because there is no “us” server to send it to. Naming a place or drawing a road route asks Apple Maps, so those coordinates go to Apple. Roamix has no account, collects no data, shows no ads, and never tracks you. Everything below simply spells that out.

Who this policy is from

Roamix is an independent app made by Maxwell Commer. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device. It applies to the Roamix iOS app and nothing else.

If anything here is unclear, please write to: maxwell.commer@gmail.com

The one thing to know: we collect nothing

Roamix has no servers, no backend, and no company database. We never receive, see, store, sell, rent, or share your personal information, because it never leaves your device to reach us in the first place. In App Store terms, Roamix is marked “Data Not Collected,” and “Tracking” is answered “No.”

There is no sign-up and no account. You do not create a profile, and we do not know who you are.

What Roamix reads on your device

To turn your real trips into maps and flythroughs, Roamix asks your permission to read a few things. Each is used only on your iPhone, and only for the feature it powers:

  • Photos — with your permission, Roamix reads the date and location (the EXIF metadata) attached to your photos so it can place them on your travel map and gather them into a trip. Roamix keeps a reference to each photo in your library, never a copy, and does not upload your photos anywhere.
  • Location — Roamix uses your location to record a trip you are taking right now. If you turn on automatic trip tracking, it also uses your location in the background so it can quietly detect and map getaways without you pressing a button. Background location is entirely optional and off until you enable it.
  • Health — with your permission, Roamix reads your workout routes (for example a run, walk, or ride) so it can replay the paths you actually travelled. Health data is read on-device and never leaves it.
  • Trips you record — when you record a live trip, the route you travel is saved on your device as part of that trip.

You are always free to grant only some of these and skip the rest — Roamix still works with whatever you choose to share, and simply leaves out the features that need what you didn’t.

Where your information lives

Everything Roamix builds — your trips, routes, place names, photo references, and stats — is stored locally on your device using Apple’s on-device database. It stays there. Because there is no Roamix server, none of it is transmitted to us or to any third party for our benefit.

Two things do leave the device, and both go to Apple rather than to us: your own private iCloud, if you use sync, and the coordinates Roamix sends to Apple Maps to get a place name or a road route back. Both are described below.

Optional iCloud sync

If you use iCloud, Roamix can keep your trips in sync across your own Apple devices using CloudKit and your private iCloud container (iCloud.com.roamix.app). This is your personal, Apple-operated iCloud storage, tied to your Apple Account — the same place your other iCloud data lives.

This matters for your privacy: even with sync on, your data goes only to your own private iCloud, governed by Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy. Maxwell Commer has no access to your iCloud container and receives nothing. If you would rather not sync, you can turn iCloud off for Roamix in the system Settings app, or simply not use iCloud.

Place names and routes come from Apple Maps

Roamix draws its maps with Apple Maps (MapKit), the mapping service built into iOS. Three things also ask Apple a question while a trip is being built: turning a coordinate into a place name, finding what is at a stop so the pin gets the right mark, and snapping a drive or a walk onto the real road. Each sends the coordinates involved to Apple, and Apple sends back a name or a route.

This happens only while Roamix is reading a trip in, as a small number of paced lookups rather than a live feed, and it does not happen at all when you are offline — a trip simply keeps its coordinates and is named later. Apple handles those requests as the map provider, under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. Nothing goes to Maxwell Commer; there is no Roamix server to send it to, and no identity is attached, because Roamix doesn’t have one for you.

Map data is © Apple and its data providers, and is shown with the attribution Apple requires.

No ads, no tracking, no selling

Roamix contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers, and no “identifier for advertisers.” We do not build a profile of you, follow you across apps or websites, or sell or share any information about you. There is nothing to sell — we don’t collect it.

Children’s privacy

Roamix is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. Because Roamix does not collect any personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, please get in touch at the address below.

You’re in control of every permission

You can review or revoke any permission you granted at any time — Roamix will keep working, minus the features that relied on it:

  • Photos — Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Roamix (or Settings › Apps › Roamix › Photos).
  • Location — Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Roamix, where you can switch between “While Using,” “Always,” or “Never.”
  • Health — open the Health app › Sharing › Apps › Roamix to change what Roamix may read.
  • iCloud sync — Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Saved to iCloud, where you can turn Roamix off.
  • Automatic trip tracking — switch it off any time inside Roamix’s own Settings.

Deleting your information

Because your data lives on your device, you can remove it yourself. Deleting a trip inside Roamix removes it from the app. Deleting the Roamix app removes its on-device store entirely. If you had iCloud sync on, you can also delete Roamix’s data from iCloud in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with a new effective date at the top, and the current version will always be available at https://maxwellcommer-hub.github.io/roamix/privacy. Because Roamix has no way to contact you, please check there for the latest version.
