Terms of Sale

Roamix is free, and every feature in it stays unlocked. The one thing you can pay for is a plaque: a real object, made by hand from one of your trips and posted to you. These terms are the agreement for buying one. They sit alongside the Roamix Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and they apply only when you order a plaque.

Who you are buying from

Plaques are made and sold by Maxwell Commer, the independent maker of Roamix, in the United States. Each one is printed, painted, assembled and packed by hand. Questions about an order go to roamix@maxwellcommer.com — please include your order code. It is shown in the app when you order and on your Stripe receipt.

What you are buying

A two-piece trip plaque, about 178 × 149 mm (7 × 5⅞ in):

  • a white PLA relief of your trip's terrain, and
  • a colour piece — the road, the stops and your title, stencil-cut — that press-fits up through it.

You choose one colour for the colour piece: Spray red, Sky, Yellow, Green or Black.

The plaque is made from the design you approve in the app: its title, subtitle, colour and map scope, shown in the preview before you pay. Please check the spelling of your title. We print what the preview shows.

Because each plaque is printed in layers and painted by hand, small marks are part of the object: fine layer lines, slight variation in the spray finish, and colours that differ a little from your screen. These are not defects. The terrain is drawn from public elevation data and your route is simplified to fit the plaque, so it is a keepsake of a trip, not a survey-accurate map.

A plaque is a decorative object, not a toy. Keep the small loose piece away from young children until it is pressed home.

When it ships

Every plaque is made to order, so nothing is waiting on a shelf.

  • We aim to ship within 10 business days of your order. Business days are Monday to Friday, not counting US federal holidays.
  • USPS then takes 2 to 5 business days to deliver it. You will get the tracking number by email when it ships.

If we can't ship within 30 days of your order, we will email you a new ship date. US law then lets you choose to cancel instead, and if you do, we refund you. If we can't make your plaque at all, we cancel and refund you. Those are the only refunds we give.

No refunds, no cancellations and no returns. Every plaque is made just for you, from your own trip and title, so once you have paid the order can't be cancelled, and we never take a plaque back. Please check your design, your title's spelling and your shipping address before you pay.
